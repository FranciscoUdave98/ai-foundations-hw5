3 · When one agent with tools would have been simpler

What would have been simpler as one agent with tools and why?

One agent is enough when…

  • It's a straight lookup, like #102: read the lease, check the balance, prepare one payment. One agent with get_lease_details get_cash_balance could do it in a single loop.
  • That means fewer model calls and handoffs, so it's cheaper, faster, and there's less to audit.
  • Questions like "what's the price of the cap?" or "how much is in checking?" don't need a meeting.

A team earns its cost when…

  • The ticket crosses departments (stock + vendor + cash + customer), like #101 and #103.
  • Roles have different authority: Accounting prepares money, Customer Service talks to customers, and only a human signs. Separate agents keep those lines clean.
  • Each specialist sees only its own tools, so mistakes stay contained and every step is easy to trace in the audit trail.

My take: route simple lookups to one agent, and save the full team for tickets that need several departments to agree.

5 · Three problems it can't solve yet, and what it would need

These need data the database doesn't have, so they need new tools and agents.

1 Forecasting demand to restock

Knowing how many hoodies to reorder before move-in week. We only have today's stock, with no sales history.

Data: an orders / order_items table with dates.
Tools: get_sales_history(sku, period)forecast_demand(sku, weeks)suggest_reorder(sku)
Agent: a Planning agent that turns forecasts into PO drafts for Accounting to cost and a human to sign.

2 "Where's my order?"

Checking delivery status for a customer. We know the vendors (even QuickShip CT, the courier), but not their shipments.

Data: a shipments table or the courier's tracking API.
Tools: get_order_status(order_id)track_shipment(tracking_no)
Agent: a Logistics agent that Sunny can ask, so customers get real ETAs instead of guesses.

3 What's actually popular?

Ranking products by past purchases to decide what to feature, discount, or drop. No purchase data exists today.

Data: purchase history by product, size, and date.
Tools: top_products(period, category)sell_through_rate(sku)
Agent: a Merchandising analyst agent that reports to Coach Dan and works with Penny on margins before any price change.
